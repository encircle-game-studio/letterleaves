Letter Leaves

An English learning game for children aged 5 to 11. Children learn words, spell them, say them out loud, build sentences and put short stories together, and a willow garden grows with everything they learn. For iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets.

No ads, no accounts, nothing collected. Everything stays on your device.

Made by Encircle Technologies Private Limited, Rajkot, India.

Help for parents

  • The parent PIN is set when you first open the app. It opens the parent area, the Shop and all settings. Forgot it? Tap "Forgot PIN?" and answer two grown-up questions.
  • Speaking practice: in the parent area choose "Turn on listening" or "Listen and repeat". Listening works on the device only. If your device cannot recognise speech offline for your English, the app uses Listen and repeat.
  • Which English (US, UK, India, Australia, Canada, Ireland, New Zealand, South Africa or other) changes spelling, everyday words and the voice. Change it in the parent area.
  • New phone: your device backup usually brings progress across. You can also save a backup file in the parent area and load it on the new device, including between iPhone and Android.
  • Purchases: levels 1 to 3 are free; open more in the Shop. Use Restore purchases in the Shop on a new device.
