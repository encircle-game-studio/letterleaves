Letter Leaves

Every word your child learns grows their own garden

An English learning game for children aged 5 to 11. Children learn words, spell them, say them out loud, build sentences and put short stories together, and a garden with a letter tree grows with everything they learn. For iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets.

No ads, no accounts, nothing collected. Everything stays on your device.

Features

An English spelling, phonics and reading game for kids aged 5 to 11, where every word your child learns grows their own garden. It plays offline, needs no account and collects nothing.

A garden that grows

Words become sprouts, sentences become flowers, and stories grow new branches on a letter tree with a leaf for every letter from A to Z.

Spell, say and read

Your child hears, spells and says words, then builds sentences and short stories, in a short mixed set of 8 games that changes each day.

Phonics step by step

Spelling follows phonics patterns, from short vowels and three-letter (CVC) words to prefixes, suffixes, homophones and silent letters.

Not yet, never wrong

A wrong answer is a "not yet": a hint is spoken, the answer is taught, and the word comes back later, with no buzzers, no lives to lose and no timers for young children.

Six gardens, ages 5 to 11

Each of the six levels is a garden of its own, and a short start check places children aged 7 and up at the right level.

For parents

No ads, no accounts, no tracking and no chat, nothing is collected, it plays offline, and a parent PIN protects the settings, the Shop and every link.

Help for parents

  • The parent PIN is made the first time a grown-up opens the Shop. Until then a grown-up check opens the parent area and all settings. Forgot it? Tap "Forgot PIN?" and answer two grown-up questions.
  • Speaking practice: in the parent area choose "Turn on listening" or "Listen and repeat". Listening works on the device only. If your device cannot recognise speech offline for your English, the app uses Listen and repeat.
  • Which English (US, UK, India, Australia, Canada, Ireland, New Zealand, South Africa or other) changes spelling, everyday words and the voice. Change it in the parent area.
  • New phone: your device backup usually brings progress across. You can also save a backup file in the parent area and load it on the new device, including between iPhone and Android.
  • Purchases: levels 1 to 3 are free; open more in the Shop. Use Restore purchases in the Shop on a new device.

Questions and answers

What is Letter Leaves?

An English spelling, phonics and reading game for children. Kids hear, spell and say words, then build sentences and short stories, and each right answer grows their own garden.

What ages is Letter Leaves for?

Children aged 5 to 11, across six levels. Younger children hear every instruction and get bigger buttons. A short start check places children aged 7 and up at the right level.

Is Letter Leaves free? Does it have ads?

It is free to download, with no ads and no subscription. Levels 1 to 3 are included. A parent can open levels 4, 5 and 6 with a one-time in-app purchase, behind a parent PIN.

Does Letter Leaves work offline?

Yes. It plays offline and progress stays on the device. On Android, speaking practice needs Android 12 or newer. Where speech cannot be checked on the device, the app uses Listen and repeat.

What data does Letter Leaves collect about my child?

None. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The microphone works only if a parent turns listening on, and speech is checked on the device. It is never recorded or sent.
