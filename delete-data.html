Delete your Word Willow data

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Word Willow, made by Encircle Technologies Private Limited, keeps everything on your device. We hold no data about you or your child, so there is nothing to delete on our side. Here is how to remove it from your devices.

One child

Open the app, tap the settings button, enter the parent PIN, then in Children tap Delete next to the child (tap again to confirm). Their progress is removed from this device.

Everything

  • iPhone and iPad: delete the app (touch and hold the icon, Remove App, Delete App). This removes all progress and settings. A copy may remain in an older iCloud or computer backup until that backup is replaced; you can manage backups in Settings › your name › iCloud.
  • Android: uninstall the app. This removes all progress and settings. A copy of the progress file may remain in your Google account backup; you can manage it in your Google account's backup settings or at drive.google.com under Backups.
  • Backup files you saved yourself from the parent area: delete them wherever you stored them.

Purchases

Purchases are kept by Apple or Google with your store account, so you can restore them. We do not store them. To ask about a purchase or a refund, contact Apple or Google.

Questions

Email support@encircletechnologies.com. We confirm in writing, within 7 days, that we hold no data for you.
