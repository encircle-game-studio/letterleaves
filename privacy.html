Letter Leaves · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Letter Leaves ("the app") is an English learning game for children aged 5 to 11, made by Encircle Technologies Private Limited, Rajkot, Gujarat, India ("we", "us"). This policy is written for parents and carers. It covers the app on iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets.

The short version

  • We collect no personal data. Nothing your child does in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no chat.
  • The app does not connect to the internet for anything of its own. On Android it does not even ask for internet access.
  • If a grown-up turns on listening, the microphone is used only to check the words your child says. This happens on the phone or tablet itself. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent.

What stays on your device

To work, the app keeps the following on your device only:

  • Up to four child profiles: a nickname (picked from a list or typed by a grown-up), an animal picture and an age (5 to 11, not a birthday).
  • Learning progress: words practised and learned, sentences and stories finished, seeds, streak days and best times.
  • Settings: which English (for example UK, US or India), sound, music, vibration, reading font, text size and the speaking-practice choice.
  • The parent PIN, stored only as a scrambled code (a salted hash), never as the PIN itself.

Deleting a child in the parent area removes that child's progress. Uninstalling the app removes everything.

Microphone and speech recognition

In Say It and Read Aloud, your child can say words and sentences out loud. The microphone is only used if a grown-up chooses "Turn on listening" in the app (behind the parent PIN) and then allows it when the device asks. You can choose "Listen and repeat" instead, which never uses the microphone.

When listening is on, the device's own speech recognition runs on the device (on iPhone and iPad with on-device recognition only; on Android with the on-device recognizer). If on-device recognition is not available, the app does not listen and uses "Listen and repeat" instead; it never sends speech to a server. The sound is used for a few seconds to check the word and is then discarded. Nothing is recorded or stored, and the words heard are not saved. No audio, voice recording or voiceprint is kept, and Letter Leaves never tries to recognise who is speaking.

On Android 13 and newer, if the device does not yet have offline speech for the chosen English, a grown-up can ask the device to download it. The device downloads it from its own speech service; Letter Leaves has no internet access and sends nothing.

The voice that reads words aloud is your device's built-in voice, also on the device.

Purchases

Levels 1 to 3 are free. Levels 4, 5 and 6 can be bought in the Shop, which is behind the parent PIN. Purchases are made with Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), under their terms. We never see your name, email or payment details. The app only receives the store's signed confirmation on the device that a level was bought, so it can open that level. If your family uses Ask to Buy (Apple) or purchase approval (Google Family Link), a purchase waits until you approve it.

Backups

  • Your device's own backup (iCloud Backup on iPhone and iPad, Google backup on Android) may include the app's progress, so it can come back on a new device. This is handled by Apple or Google under your account; we cannot see it. On Android only the progress file is included.
  • In the parent area you can save a backup file yourself and load it on another device. The file goes wherever you choose to put it. It contains the progress listed above and the scrambled PIN. We never receive it.

Children's privacy

The app is made for children and is listed in the App Store Kids category and in the Google Play Families programme. Because we collect no personal information from children, there is nothing for a parent to consent to under laws such as COPPA (United States), GDPR (European Union and United Kingdom) and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. The app has no tracking, no behavioural monitoring and no advertising of any kind. Links out of the app, the Shop and the settings are behind a parent gate.

Age signals from the app store

Where the law requires it (for example in Texas, Utah and Louisiana), the app asks the App Store or Google Play for the age range the store shares with apps. It uses that range only on your device, for the current session, to explain in the Shop that a purchase needs approval from the family organiser, and (on iPhone and iPad) to ask a parent to approve the update if the app's age rating ever changes. The app never stores the age range or sends it anywhere.

India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)

Encircle Technologies Private Limited is the Data Fiduciary for the app. The app processes no personal data of children or adults on our side, so no notice-and-consent request is shown. If you have a question or a complaint, write to our grievance contact, support@encircletechnologies.com. We will reply within 7 days and at most within 90 days. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India. A Hindi version is being prepared; this policy in Hindi or any other language listed in the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution is available on request.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what personal data we hold about you or your child, and ask us to correct or delete it. We hold none, and we will confirm that in writing if you ask. See Delete your Letter Leaves data for removing data from your devices.

Changes

If we ever change what the app does with data, we will update this policy and the date above before the change reaches the app, and we will update the App Store and Google Play privacy information at the same time.
